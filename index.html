About

One shoot,
everything covered

I'm a Big Island photographer and editor who shoots listing media — stills, 3D tours, floor plans and video. The point is that an agent books once and gets the whole package back, instead of chasing three vendors and three timelines for one listing.

I came up through video, so the moving pieces are the part I actually enjoy: the walkthrough that shows how a house flows, the vertical cut that performs on a feed, the tour a buyer on the mainland can get lost in at midnight.

Rates are competitive because I'm building the book right now, and I'd rather work with agents who care about how their listings look. Everything comes back the next business day — that part isn't negotiable.
